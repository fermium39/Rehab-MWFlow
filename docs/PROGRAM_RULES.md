# Rehab MW-Flow — Program Rules
Version: draft 0.1 (2026-11-02)
Status: DESIGN RULES ONLY — not a prescribed or approved weekly schedule.

## Scope
- Build symptom-aware, low-impact exercise sessions. Do not limit the exercise library to the existing illustration set.
- Target session duration: 30–60 minutes, adjustable to tolerance and capacity.
- Each training session should contain an appropriate warm-up, mobility/activation, main exercise, and cool-down; emphasis and dose vary by day.
- Include recovery days, gradual progression, and alternatives. Do not require every exercise every day.
- Strength training should specify sets, reps, rest, tempo or hold, equipment and load, modifications, and stop conditions.
- The program must be reviewed and explicitly approved before replacing the existing schedule in the app.

## Safety rules
- Do not train through worsening joint pain, sharp pain, instability, new swelling, numbness, or radiating/neurological symptoms.
- Avoid high-impact exercise and deep loaded knee flexion when knee symptoms are active. Reassess tolerance rather than imposing permanent prohibitions.
- Favor controlled, supported movements and gradual load increases. No assumption that a given kettlebell or dumbbell weight is safe for all exercises.
- Consider evaluation by a qualified clinician/physiotherapist for persistent or worsening knee pain, radiating back pain, weakness, or red flags.
- These rules are not a diagnosis or individualized medical clearance.

## Progression / regression
- Record symptoms before, during, and after sessions.
- Regress range, resistance, volume, or duration when symptoms worsen; stop when red flags appear.
- Increase one training variable at a time after consistently tolerating current workload.
- Provide alternatives for symptomatic days and rest/recovery options.

## Content governance
- Public repository: generic training logic, exercise metadata, schema, references, version history.
- Private information (personal symptoms, body measurements, individual equipment inventory, clinical history) must NOT be committed to this public repository.
- Keep workout-plan drafts separate from the currently deployed plan until user approval.
- Create new exercise illustrations only after the revised program and exercises are approved.

## Evidence and source policy
- For each exercise or program recommendation, record source title, issuing organization/author, URL, access date, and scope/limitations in SOURCES.md.
- Prefer established clinical/rehabilitation and exercise-science sources. Do not present generic guidance as an individualized medical prescription.
